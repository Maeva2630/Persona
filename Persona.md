# Persona — User Manual

## What is Persona?
Persona is a personalized AI-powered newsletter assistant. You interact with it via a chatbot, and it sends you a customized newsletter based on your interests at the time you choose.

---

## Getting Started - Workflow 1

### Register as a Regular User
Talk to the chatbot and say **"I want to register"**.

The bot will ask you for:
1. **Username** — your unique identifier
2. **Password** — kept secure and hashed
3. **Email** — where your newsletter will be sent
4. **Topics** — your interests (e.g. AI, sports, politics, gaming)
5. **Send time** — when you want to receive your newsletter (e.g. 08:00, 18:00)
6. **Newsletter style** — choose one:
   - Concise and neutral
   - Detailed and formal
   - Casual and fun
   - Bullet points only
   - Short summaries

You will then be shown our privacy notice and asked for your **GDPR consent**. Without consent, no account can be created.

### Register as an Advertiser
Say **"I want to register as an advertiser"**.

The bot will ask you for:
1. **Username**
2. **Password**
3. **Email**
4. **Company name**

---

## Logging In
Say **"I want to login"**.

The bot will ask for your username, password, and whether you are a regular user or advertiser.

---

## Regular User Features

### Update Your Preferences
Once logged in, you can update any of your settings:
- **"Change my email to x"**
- **"Change my topics to x"**
- **"Change my send time to x"**
- **"Change my password"**
- **"Change my newsletter style to x"**

### Subscribe / Unsubscribe
- **"Unsubscribe"** — stop receiving newsletters
- **"Resubscribe"** — start receiving newsletters again

### View Your Profile
- **"Show my profile"** — displays your current preferences

### Public Profile
- **"Make my profile public"** — other users can see your username, topics and newsletter style
- **"Make my profile private"** — your profile is hidden (default)

### View Another User's Profile
- Type **"@username"** — shows their public profile if they made it public

### Submit Feedback
- **"I didn't like the third article"**
- **"Please don't use this source in the future"**
- **"I want more articles about X"**

Your feedback will be used to improve future newsletters.

### Delete Your Account
- **"Delete my account"** — permanently deletes all your data
- The bot will ask for confirmation before deleting

---

## Advertiser Features

### Submit an Ad
Once logged in as an advertiser, say **"I want to submit an ad"**.

The bot will ask for:
1. **Ad content** — the text of your ad
2. **Target topics** — which users to target (e.g. "AI, sports, tech")

Your ad will appear in the newsletters of users interested in those topics.

---

## Admin Features
Admin accounts are assigned manually by the system administrator.

Admins can:
- **List all users** — "show all users"
- **Delete any account** — "delete account of username"

---

## Security

- Your password is never stored in plain text
- You must be logged in to access any feature
- Simply providing a username does not authenticate you
